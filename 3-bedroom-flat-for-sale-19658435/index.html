A bright & spacious three bedroom apartment in Warwick Court, with super balcony views.

This spacious three bedroom apartment enjoys a quiet position in this development on Northlands Drive, with leafy balcony views over the communal garden. Having been a successful rental property for many years, the property has tenants in situ until September 2026, and should now suit investors and owner occupiers.

The layout consists of three well-proportioned bedrooms, all currently hosting a double bed, and a modern family sized bathroom. The main living room is a bright space, with large windows bringing light in. The kitchen is separate, providing plenty of storage cupboards and preparation worktop space. There is a good sized lobby leading to the living room, and a private balcony to enjoy the views - southerly in aspect.

The apartment has double glazing, electric heating and half a shared double garage, which could house a car or be used for storage. Access into Winchester city centre is straight forward, and there are regular buses on the Worthy Road connecting the area to Kings Worthy and beyond. With lovely parkland walks moment away, as well as the Theatre Royal, library, cafes and restaurants, the area is most practical & central. Please contact Goadsby to arrange your viewing.
