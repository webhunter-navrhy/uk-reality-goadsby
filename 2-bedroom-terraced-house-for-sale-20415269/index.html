Description

A TWO BEDROOM TERRACED PROPERTY IN HARNHAM WITH PARKING

This charming home on Halfpenny Road is a testament to careful maintenance and stylish enhancement by its current owners. If you're looking for a two-bedroom house in a convenient Salisbury location that offers more than the standard specification, this is a must-see. The property's location, well-appointed interior, and lovely garden make it an excellent choice for first-time buyers, downsizers, and investors alike.

The ground floor, which benefits from solid wood flooring, comprises a handy cloakroom and a contemporary kitchen featuring a range of base and eye-level cabinetry, a dark laminate work surface, and space for full range of white goods. The sitting/dining room is a welcoming space for relaxing or entertaining, with double patio doors that connect the interior seamlessly with the rear garden.

Upstairs, the property offers two well-proportioned bedrooms, served by a bathroom that has been modernised to a high standard. The bathroom features striking green metro-style tiling on the walls around the bath and shower, a modern white vanity unit, and matching white sanitaryware.

The exterior of the property is equally well-considered. To the rear, double patio doors from the sitting room open onto a paved patio area, which is perfect for an introduction to the landscaped rear garden. The garden features a mature and thoughtful selection of flora and greenery, set within a well-maintained lawn. A second patio area at the far end of the garden, provides an ideal spot for al fresco seating. The garden is enclosed by timber panel fencing. A timber side gate offers convenient access to the rear of the plot. Allocated parking is available adjacent to the property.

Positioned to provide excellent access to Salisbury city centre, the property is located in an area well-known for scenic pedestrian routes, including the Harnham Town Path. Residents can enjoy easy access to Salisbury's wide range of amenities, including high street shops, supermarkets, primary and secondary schools, leisure facilities, and a variety of restaurants, pubs, and bars. For those looking further afield, the property offers great road links to Dorset, the New Forest, and the coastal cities of Bournemouth and Southampton. The Salisbury train station provides direct routes to London Waterloo and the West Country.

Further benefits include gas fired central heating and UPVC Double Glazing. Council Tax Band C.
